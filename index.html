Welcome

Christian S. Lopez, MD proudly launched CSL Cardiology, PLLC, his practice dedicated to optimizing your cardiovascular health. A comprehensive yet individualized, compassionate yet objective, caring, and overall proactive approach to maximizing your cardiovascular longevity. His patient-centered approach is meant to empower you with the knowledge and preventative strategies to enhance each stage of your cardiovascular life.

Through the trust and confidence Dr. Lopez has earned from his patients over the years, he remains dedicated to providing you with that same level of commitment, dependability, and support in your “heart-health journey.”

CSL Cardiology, PLLC provides personalized outpatient Cardiology consultation to help you better understand your overall cardiovascular health, preventative strategies consistent with a proactive approach, and patient-specific management of cardiovascular conditions that best fit your life. We are dedicated to provide the highest standards of patient care as your “heart health” is, and always will be, our utmost priority.

We welcome new and returning patients. Visits are by appointment only (Telehealth visits when appropriate). It is never too late to start and you have nothing to lose and everything to gain.
