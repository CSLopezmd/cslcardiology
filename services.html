Services Services Offered

Please contact us for a more detailed discussion of current services listed.
We are constantly updating our offerings to best optimize your overall heart health.

Cardiology Consultation

Comprehensive yet individualized cardiovascular assessments designed to thoroughly evaluate any symptom-related cardiovascular health problems/symptoms. These evaluations are complemented by carefully tailored treatment plans that are unique to each patient's specific comorbidities and familial cardiac risk factors, ensuring personalized and effective care.

Diagnostic Interpretation

Detailed analysis and explanation of cardiac test results: EKGs, Echocardiograms, stress tests, Coronary Calcium Score & soft-plaque imaging, Coronary angiograms, aorta/carotid ultrasounds, pacemaker interrogations, tilt-table-test, etc. in order to provide maximum clarity about disease diagnosis and informed care plan.

Cardiac Device Management

Comprehensive monitoring & management of patients with cardiac devices (pacemakers, wearable/internal defibrillators, Loop Recorders): monitoring, programming adjustments, and continuous support to ensure optimal device performance and patient wellbeing.

Cardiac Medication Management

Expert oversight of cardiology-specific medications and/or medication cardio-vascular side-effects.

Patient Education

Clear, accessible guidance on heart health, lifestyle changes, and disease prevention to empower you with the knowledge to maintain long-term cardiovascular wellness & “heart health.”

Preventative Care / Monitoring

Proactive management of cardiovascular risk factors towards the prevention of future major adverse events (i.e. heart attacks, strokes, aortic aneurysms, peripheral arterial/venous disease).
